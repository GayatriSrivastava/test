# 📚 Test — My Exam Error & Revision Repository

Welcome to **Test**, my personal error-rectification and revision repository for my four years of college.

This repository is where I will document the questions I answer incorrectly in weekly tests, quizzes, mid-semester exams, end-semester exams, and other assessments across all the subjects I study during college.

The goal is simple: **learn from my mistakes, identify my weak areas, and make sure I don't repeat the same mistakes in future exams.**

## 🎯 Purpose of This Repository

While preparing for exams, it is easy to forget which concepts caused difficulty in previous tests. Instead of starting revision from scratch every time, I want to maintain a record of my mistakes that I can revisit whenever an exam approaches.

This repository will help me:

- 📝 **Track my mistakes:** Maintain a record of questions I answered incorrectly in exams and tests.
- 🔍 **Identify weak areas:** Understand which topics and concepts need more attention.
- 📖 **Revise effectively:** Focus more on concepts I previously struggled with instead of revising everything equally.
- 🔁 **Avoid repeating mistakes:** Revisit incorrect questions and strengthen my understanding of the underlying concepts.
- 🎯 **Prepare strategically:** Before every exam, review previous mistakes and prioritize the topics that need extra practice.
- 📈 **Track my progress:** Observe how my understanding improves over time and how my weak areas gradually become strengths.

## 📝 What Will Each File Contain?

Each subject-specific Markdown file will contain the questions I got wrong, along with their correct answers and explanations wherever necessary.

The general format will be:

### Q1. [Question]

**Answer:** [Correct answer]

**Explanation:** [A short explanation to understand the concept and avoid the same mistake again.]

This will make the files easy to read, search, update, and revise directly on GitHub.

## 🔄 My Error-Rectification Process

Whenever I get a question wrong in a test or exam, I will follow this process:

1. **Record** the incorrect question in the appropriate subject file.
2. **Understand** the correct answer and the concept behind it.
3. **Identify** why I made the mistake or what I misunderstood.
4. **Revise** the question and related concepts regularly.
5. **Reattempt** the question without looking at the answer.
6. **Review** the file again before the next test or exam.

## 🎓 How I Will Use This Repository Before Exams

Before every major exam, I will revisit the relevant subject folders and review the questions I previously answered incorrectly.

This will help me identify recurring mistakes, recognize patterns in my weak areas, and allocate more revision time to topics that need it.

Rather than simply asking, *"What do I need to study for this exam?"*, I will also ask:

- Which concepts have I struggled with before?
- Which mistakes have I repeated?
- Which topics do I still find difficult?
- What should I practice more before the exam?

By answering these questions, I can make my revision more focused and productive.

## 🌱 My Long-Term Goal

This repository is not just a collection of incorrect answers. It is a record of my learning journey throughout my four years of college.

Every mistake I document is an opportunity to understand something better. Over time, I want this repository to become my personal revision handbook — a place where I can revisit past difficulties, measure my improvement, and prepare for exams with a better understanding of my strengths and weaknesses.

**The objective is not to never make mistakes. It is to learn from them, rectify them, and avoid making the same mistakes again.**
